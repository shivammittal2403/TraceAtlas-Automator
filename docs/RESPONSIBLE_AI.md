# Analytical boundaries

Evidence is the record; models remain advisory. This pipeline is fully
deterministic and records no hidden chain-of-thought. Claims, observations,
uncertainties, contradictions and verification are visible structured outputs.

Corroborated source assertions do not establish intent, identity, guilt or
causation. Similar names never trigger merges. Every report is a draft requiring
human release. No contact, takedown, blocking, financial transaction, external
notification or law-enforcement decision occurs. Future AI integrations must
retain these deterministic boundaries and evidence-linked evaluation.

# Authorized person records

`person` seeds are case-local public identifiers; the runner ingests
operator-approved SourceDocuments. Every fact subject must equal the authorized
seed. Public-label assertions can be corroborated as source assertions, while
identity candidates remain POSSIBLE_MATCH and canonical_merge stays false.

There is no new live person search, private profile acquisition, email/phone
harvesting or attribution. Existing sensitive workflows and ResolutionService
remain separate governed owners. Their consent controls are not bypassed by this
record-ingestion mode. A label match does not establish that two people are one.

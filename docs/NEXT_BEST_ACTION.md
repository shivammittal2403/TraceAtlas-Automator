# Next-action ranking

Current priorities are explicit rules: review overlapping contradictions
first, seek independent corroboration for incompletely supported claims second,
then human draft review. Every action includes its basis and
`automatic_execution: false`.

No arbitrary scientific score or calibrated information-gain claim is made. The
runner exhausts its permitted source list in one cycle, then stops. A deeper
research action requires an executable source adapter, exact scope and budget,
and a new approved task. Future experiments must measure added evidence value,
independence, cost, latency and policy risk against a held-out case set.
